Privacy policy

The Calculus of Kings · last updated March 2026

This page describes how the app handles information when you play offline or in the browser. There is no user account system in this product.

Data stored on your device

Game progress, settings, and related state may be saved in your browser or app storage (for example localStorage on the web, or equivalent storage in the native shell). This data stays on your device unless you clear it or uninstall the app.

What we do not collect in this build

  • No sign-in or profile database operated by the game.
  • No embedded third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in this repository build.
  • No sale of personal information.

Network

The game can run fully offline after assets are loaded. If you use a network, your connection is subject to your browser or OS and any store distribution platform you used to install the app.

Children

The game is suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what the platform stores locally as described above.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app changes. The revision date at the top will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support or contact channel listed on the app’s store page or project page for your platform.
