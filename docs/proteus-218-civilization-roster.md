# Proteus 218 Civilization Roster

The roster is fictional but grounded in geography, economy, military doctrine, and believable civic identity. Each civilization needs a fun power fantasy, a fair weakness, and a reason to fight that is not cartoon villainy.

## Aurelian Concord

Region: central river cities, road hubs, courts, old capitals.  
Power fantasy: disciplined combined arms, roads, civic order, flexible legions.  
Weakness: expensive reforms, predictable doctrine, vulnerable to road disruption.  
Signature units: Concord Shield Cohort, Road Warden Engineers, Magistrate Guard.  
Unique mechanic: **Edict Network** - connected roads and courts reduce unrest and reinforce faster, but losing hubs causes morale shocks.  
Story logic: believes river law must be enforced or civilization ends.

## Veyr Steppe Clans

Region: dry grass corridors and caravan tracks.  
Power fantasy: cavalry tempo, scouting, raids, rapid encirclement.  
Weakness: weak sieges, weaker walls, struggles in marsh and dense cities.  
Signature units: Windmane Riders, Dust Lancers, Camp Scouts.  
Unique mechanic: **Moving Hearths** - mobile supply camps let armies operate far from cities, but camps are vulnerable if found.  
Story logic: believes settled powers wasted time pretending the drought would wait.

## Kharu Basin League

Region: reservoir basins, desert wells, dry canals.  
Power fantasy: drought resilience, disciplined logistics, spear defense.  
Weakness: slow population growth, low early tempo.  
Signature units: Basin Spears, Cistern Guard, Ledger Quartermasters.  
Unique mechanic: **Water Ledger** - precise rationing reduces drought penalties and army supply loss, but emergency spending creates civic anger.  
Story logic: believes survival requires counting water before sentiment.

## Thessari Academies

Region: hill academies, observatories, engineer cities.  
Power fantasy: research, commanders, siege precision, formation science.  
Weakness: fragile early economy and expensive specialists.  
Signature units: Theorem Sappers, Academy Ballistae, Proofguard Infantry.  
Unique mechanic: **Solved Problems** - research unlocks precise counters after scouting enemy doctrine.  
Story logic: believes the old compact failed because leaders ignored evidence.

## Maro Reed Cities

Region: marsh deltas, reed belts, variable channels.  
Power fantasy: ambush, skirmishers, hidden routes, water denial.  
Weakness: poor heavy cavalry and weaker open-field staying power.  
Signature units: Reed Runners, Marsh Slingers, Silent Punts as non-combat logistics flavor only.  
Unique mechanic: **Hidden Channels** - marsh regions conceal movement and improve skirmisher retreats.  
Story logic: believes local water knowledge must not be surrendered to empires.

## Durnic Ironholds

Region: mountain quarries, fortress passes, iron roads.  
Power fantasy: elite infantry, armor, fortresses, attrition.  
Weakness: slow expansion, poor farms, predictable routes.  
Signature units: Iron Oath Infantry, Gatebreakers, Stonewright Engineers.  
Unique mechanic: **Holdfasts** - mountain cities become extremely hard to crack when supplied, but each new expansion is slow and costly.  
Story logic: believes only hard borders and stored strength survive collapse.

## Selene Orchard Principalities

Region: terraced orchards, temperate farms, festival cities.  
Power fantasy: food, morale, recovery, archers, resilient campaigns.  
Weakness: iron scarcity and weaker armor.  
Signature units: Orchard Archers, Laurel Shields, Harvest Wardens.  
Unique mechanic: **Seasonal Stores** - strong grain reserves and recovery, but drought years force painful ration choices.  
Story logic: believes civilization is worth preserving because ordinary life is worth defending.

## Oron Red Marches

Region: badlands, salt roads, exposed frontiers.  
Power fantasy: cheap mass, shock infantry, intimidation, early conquest.  
Weakness: order volatility, weak water infrastructure, collapse risk after overreach.  
Signature units: Red March Axes, Salt Road Levies, Banner Breakers.  
Unique mechanic: **Fear Momentum** - rapid victories reduce enemy cohesion and speed recruitment, but defeats trigger unrest.  
Story logic: believes the old river powers treated the marches as expendable.

## Ilyr Canal Wardens

Region: canals, locks, bridge cities, irrigation districts.  
Power fantasy: engineers, crossings, fieldworks, defensive mobility.  
Weakness: high infrastructure dependency and engineer vulnerability.  
Signature units: Lock Engineers, Canal Spears, Bridge Wardens.  
Unique mechanic: **Gatecraft** - build, close, open, reinforce, or sabotage crossings faster than anyone.  
Story logic: believes water infrastructure gives them responsibility others mistake for arrogance.

## Nadir Free Camps

Region: displaced frontiers, refugee camps, broken trade roads.  
Power fantasy: adaptation, mercenary variety, salvage, counter-drafting.  
Weakness: no single elite identity and fragile cohesion.  
Signature units: Free Camp Auxiliaries, Salvage Crews, Oathless Riders.  
Unique mechanic: **Borrowed Doctrine** - adapt after fighting or scouting enemies; powerful late but inconsistent early.  
Story logic: believes the old nations already failed the people now living between flags.
