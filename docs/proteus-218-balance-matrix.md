# Proteus 218 Balance Matrix

This file is a quick reference for unit, civilization, and strategy balance. It should be updated whenever Proteus gameplay changes.

## Unit counter matrix

| Unit class | Wins when | Loses when | Best terrain | Worst terrain | Strategic cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Shield Infantry | Holding lines, protecting archers/engines, fighting at crossings | Flanked, exhausted, kited by missiles, isolated from command | Crossings, roads, city approaches | Marsh, broken hills | Expensive training and upkeep |
| Spear Infantry | Braced against cavalry, holding chokepoints, protecting siege | Disrupted, surrounded, worn down by skirmishers | Crossings, ridges, narrow passes | Open steppe if unsupported | Low mobility and weak pursuit |
| Sword Infantry | Urban assault, flexible melee, exploiting broken lines | Charging braced spears or shield walls unsupported | City streets, dry beds, road fights | Open cavalry ground | Requires timing and morale support |
| Skirmishers | Harassing heavy troops, scouting, marsh fighting, picking off engineers | Caught in melee or cavalry pursuit | Marsh, scrub, broken ground | Open roads near cavalry | Low staying power |
| Archers/Slingers | Morale pressure, softening slow formations, defending walls | Unscreeened, low supply, bad weather/dust | Ridges, walls, rear lines | Dense marsh, close urban lanes | Needs protection and ammunition |
| Cavalry | Scouting, raids, flanks, pursuit, tempo control | Braced spears, marshes, walls, narrow crossings | Steppe, roads, dry plains | Marsh, city, bridge choke | Weak at taking fortified ground |
| Engineers | Building crossings, roads, fieldworks, saps, repairs, sabotage | Direct combat, raids, poor escorting | Roads, riverbanks, siege lines | Exposed open ground | Delays raw military mass |
| Siege Engines | Breaking walls, forcing fights, morale shock | Raids, bad roads, no infantry screen | Roads, prepared siege lines | Marsh, hills without roads | Slow and supply-hungry |
| Command Guard | Stabilizing morale, anchoring formations, carrying abilities | Overexposed, surrounded, isolated | Center line, defensible roads | Deep raids without support | Losing it creates army shock |

## Civilization balance snapshots

| Civilization | Power fantasy | Primary counterplay | Timing window | Failure mode |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Aurelian Concord | Professional combined arms and road reinforcement | Disrupt roads, force unpredictable terrain fights, out-specialize | Midgame after road network | Overpaying for reforms while fronts multiply |
| Veyr Steppe Clans | Cavalry tempo and map-wide pressure | Fortified crossings, spear screens, protected supply hubs | Early/mid before heavy walls | Failing to end before sieges matter |
| Kharu Basin League | Drought resilience and logistics discipline | Force multi-front speed, raid reservoirs, avoid attrition traps | Mid/late drought phases | Slow growth and low initiative |
| Thessari Academies | Tech, commanders, siege precision | Early pressure, cavalry raids, sabotage workshops | Midgame tech spike | Fragile early economy |
| Maro Reed Cities | Ambush, marsh warfare, water denial | Force open-field fights, secure roads, burn tempo with engineers | Any marsh-heavy map | Poor heavy staying power |
| Durnic Ironholds | Fortresses, armor, grinding siege | Starve expansions, bypass passes, exploit food limits | Late after armor/stone economy | Slow expansion and weak farms |
| Selene Orchard Principalities | Food, morale, recovery, archers | Deny iron, raid terraces, force fast shock battles | Long games | Under-armored armies |
| Oron Red Marches | Cheap mass and shock tempo | Survive first waves, trigger unrest, punish water shortages | Early aggression | Order collapse after overreach |
| Ilyr Canal Wardens | Engineers and battlefield shaping | Kill engineers, force fights away from infrastructure | Midgame infrastructure | Dependency on built networks |
| Nadir Free Camps | Adaptation and counter-drafting | Deny salvage, force specialization, win before adaptation | Reactive after scouting | No universal elite core |

## Balance heuristics

- No faction should have both the best early rush and the best late siege.
- No unit should beat its counter on neutral terrain without support, veterancy, commander advantage, or resource investment.
- Drought should change priorities but should not randomly decide matches.
- Total victory should require both battlefield success and strategic follow-through.
- Capturing cities should create obligations: supply strain, unrest, road defense, and water management.
- Every strong tactic needs a visible warning and at least one reliable response.

## Strategy archetypes

| Archetype | How it wins | How it loses | Best civilizations |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Timing push | Hits before opponent's tree/economy matures | Misses window and falls behind | Veyr, Oron, Thessari |
| Fortified attrition | Holds water/roads until enemy overextends | Gets bypassed or out-expanded | Kharu, Durnic, Ilyr |
| Combined-arms conquest | Uses balanced armies to solve many fronts | Gets out-tempoed or out-specialized | Aurelian, Selene |
| Logistics strangulation | Cuts roads, crossings, reservoirs, and reinforcements | Fails to convert pressure into captures | Veyr, Maro, Nadir |
| Siege inevitability | Protects engines and cracks capitals | Loses supply lines or takes too long | Durnic, Thessari, Ilyr |
| Adaptive counterplay | Scouts, counters, and pivots cheaper than rivals | Lacks a decisive win condition | Nadir, Aurelian |
