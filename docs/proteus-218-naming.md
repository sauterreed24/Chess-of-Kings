# Proteus 218 Naming Options

## Recommended title

**Proteus 218: Crown of the Last River**

Why it works:

- Keeps the memorable planet name.
- Communicates conquest and total victory through “Crown.”
- Centers the colossal river and drought premise.
- Sounds premium without being generic.

## Strong alternatives

1. **Proteus 218: The Last River** - clean, dramatic, slightly less war-forward.
2. **Proteus 218: Drought of Kings** - connects to Chess-of-Kings branding, but more abstract.
3. **Proteus 218: Riverfall** - short and marketable, but less clear.
4. **Proteus 218: Broken Compact** - excellent story title, weaker as a store title.
5. **Proteus 218: Crowns of Dust** - evocative and tragic, slightly fantasy leaning.
6. **The Aurel Compact** - sophisticated, but loses the sci-fi planet identity.
7. **Crown of the Last River** - strongest standalone title if dropping the numeric planet prefix.

## Naming rules for factions

- Names should sound plausible, not random fantasy syllables.
- Names should imply geography, institution, or history.
- Avoid direct copies of real civilizations.
- Avoid turning one faction into obvious villains through harsh naming alone.

## Current faction name assessment

- Aurelian Concord: strong central-law identity.
- Veyr Steppe Clans: fast and regional; “Veyr” is short and readable.
- Kharu Basin League: scarcity/accounting/water identity.
- Thessari Academies: intellectual hill-city flavor.
- Maro Reed Cities: marsh and local-knowledge flavor.
- Durnic Ironholds: mountain/fortress/iron clarity.
- Selene Orchard Principalities: food, culture, morale identity.
- Oron Red Marches: frontier anger and badland identity.
- Ilyr Canal Wardens: infrastructure and gatekeeping identity.
- Nadir Free Camps: displaced people between nations.
